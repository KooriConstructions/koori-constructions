## 1. Position Description

**Title:** Residential Estimator (Sales Estimator) · **Reports to:** Brad Robinson (Director)
**Location:** Central Coast / Lake Macquarie / Newcastle / Hunter (field + office)
**Type:** Full-time

**Purpose:** Own every residential quote end-to-end so Brad's off the tools of quoting — from
first site visit to sent quote to close — across the full residential range: bathrooms, kitchens,
laundries, decks, pergolas, interior/exterior renovations, extensions, new builds, granny flats,
additions — everything under residential carpentry & building construction.

**Key responsibilities**
- Run the **speed-to-lead**: contact new residential leads **within the hour**, book the site visit.
- Attend site, **measure and scope** the job, capture photos/notes.
- **Price it in Buildxact** using Koori's recipe library (KRV0/KCI0), the **pricing waterfall A→K**
  and the **margin matrix** (20% resi baseline, up for conditions) — never fabricate a rate.
- Produce the quote in Koori house format with **staged invoicing** ($15k+), send it, and
  **follow up** to close.
- Handle variations, PC-item selections, and client questions through to acceptance.
- **Run the estimating system, not fight it** — review/finish what Buildxact + the AI Chief
  Estimator draft; feed clean completed jobs back as recipes (kaizen).
- Keep the pipeline current (SharePoint register); flag anything needing Brad's sign-off.

**Must-have**
- Residential **building / carpentry background** — reads plans and *knows real costs*.
- **Residential estimating experience** (renos + new builds).
- **Buildxact** (or Databuild / Cubit / Buildern) proficiency.
- Client-facing and confident on site + on the phone; organised; follows up relentlessly.
- Systems-minded; fast **and** accurate; NSW drivers licence.

**Nice-to-have:** QS/Cert IV+ estimating; Stratco/Cooldek familiarity; insurance/NDIS scopes;
knows the CC/Newcastle supplier + subbie base.

- "Walk me through the last residential job you priced start to finish — how you measured, what
  rates you used, how you checked the margin." (probes real competence)
