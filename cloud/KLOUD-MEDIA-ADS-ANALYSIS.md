## Their content model (from the actual ad hooks)
Their OWN ads (selling their service to trades) fall into 3 hook types — classic
direct-response:
1. **Outcome/proof with a big specific $ number, niche-specific:**
   - "$1.2M in Decks in 12 months"
   - "$600k in Pergolas in 6 months"
   - "Land $50k–$200k Renovations Every Month"
2. **Pain / FOMO:**
   - "Someone Else Just Took the Job You Wanted"
   - "Stuck In The Rat Race?"
   - "This could be you"
3. **Trust / anti-agency differentiation:**
   - "Most Agencies Lie. We Just Do the Work."
- Many ads have **blank titles = video/UGC reels** (the real creative — talking-head /
  testimonial / case-study format). That's the content they produce.
